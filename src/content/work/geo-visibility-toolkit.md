Search stopped being ten blue links and became an answer. For a hotel, that changes the question from "do we rank" to "does the model know we exist, and does it repeat us correctly."

I built a front-end audit tool that scores a hotel domain on that visibility, covering how the domain is represented to answer engines and where the gaps are, then used it to shape a GEO strategy across the hotel portfolio.

Being early here is the point, and so is being honest about it: this is a pilot. I built it, I ran it, and the useful outcome so far is a repeatable way to ask the question rather than a proven lift in a metric. GEO is new enough that anyone claiming a clean before-and-after number is probably reporting noise. What I can defend is the method: structured data quality, entity consistency, and crawlability are what an answer engine actually consumes, and they are measurable today.
