## What it is

Western Drilling Tools is a Calgary manufacturer of drilling tools and turnkey CNC machining, selling into six distinct industries. The problem with a catalogue that wide is that a mining buyer and a waterwell driller want different things from the same product line.

The site is built as a matrix: an industry page and a matching product listing for each of oil and gas, construction, horizontal directional drilling, mining, waterwell and geotechnical, plus machine capabilities, services, team and a quote request. Buyers enter through their own vertical and reach a quote path without passing through the full catalogue. Site search covers the case where they already know the part.

## My part

Wireframes from the information architecture, prototypes in Figma, then the build in Webflow with custom code where the platform ran out. Content strategy came out of competitive analysis in Google Analytics, Ahrefs and Search Console, and I partnered with copywriters on the SEO-driven page copy.

*This was agency work at Catch Digital, and the site has been maintained by others since handover. The structure and build are mine; anything added after early 2021 is not.*
